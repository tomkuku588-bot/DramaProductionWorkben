Welcome to DramaProductionWorkbench (AI Drama Production Workbench). This policy applies to the HarmonyOS version of the app and explains how information is handled when you install and use it. The developer/operator is the entity publishing the app, as displayed on the AppGallery listing under the verified developer identity. The contact email is tomkuku588@gmail.com. The app is an offline creation tool that is free, ad-free, and requires no registration.

## 1. What Information We Process and Why

We process mainly the local creative data that you actively create, edit, or generate on-device while using the app, including comic drama projects, manuscripts, scripts and storylines, character/scene/prop setting assets, storyboard content, prompt text assembled locally by rules, review records, image-text delivery packages, backup files, app settings, and the statistics, validation, and display results generated locally from that data. The purposes are project organization, manuscript and script editing, setting-asset and storyboard editing, local prompt assembly, delivery-package generation, page display, and necessary troubleshooting. The current version does not provide account registration or login and does not collect your phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photo library, facial data, fingerprints, or similar personal information by default.

## 2. Storage Location, Retention, and Deletion

The above information is stored by default in a database inside the app sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded to any developer-operated server, and the current version does not provide cloud sync. Retention depends on your own actions. The relevant data can be removed when you delete creative content, clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset the device. If you later copy backup or exported files generated by the app to another device, cloud drive, or third-party service, that storage is controlled by you.

## 3. Permissions and System Capabilities

The core features of the current version run fully offline, do not depend on network access, and the app does not request sensitive system permissions such as notifications, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS. To support optional features such as manuscript import, backup, and exporting image-text delivery packages, the app may invoke HarmonyOS system capabilities such as file picking, file saving, or system sharing solely to complete the action you initiated, and the read or write scope is the file you select in the system picker. Other than what is necessary for those functions, the current version does not read unrelated files in the background and does not collect personal information unrelated to comic drama creation. If a future version adds new permissions or system capabilities, we will explain them before the relevant feature is triggered and obtain authorization where required by law.

## 4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with unrelated third parties, and we do not publicly disclose it without authorization. External transfer may occur only when you actively use system sharing, export, or backup migration features; when required by laws, regulators, or courts; or when necessary to protect the personal or property safety of you, other users, or the public. In such cases, we will take reasonable protective measures within the scope permitted by law.

## 5. Third-Party SDKs and External Services

Except for HarmonyOS base system capabilities, the current version does not integrate third-party SDKs for advertising, commercial analytics, social login, maps, payment, or push notifications, and it does not upload your manuscripts, scripts, characters, scenes, props, storyboards, or other creative data to any developer-operated server or external service. The current version of the app does not call any AI service locally or in the cloud and does not synthesize video; it only assembles prompt text on your device according to preset rules, which you may copy into external tools yourself. If a future version introduces network-based AI services or other third-party services, we will update this policy and, where necessary, obtain your explicit consent through notices, dialogs, or system authorization flows.

## 6. Data Security

We adopt reasonable safeguards appropriate to the current product form, including local processing, the minimum-necessary principle, and limiting unnecessary data access, in order to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no technical measure can guarantee absolute security. You remain responsible for the security of your device, system accounts, and any creative content files you export.

## 7. Your Rights

You can view, correct, supplement, or delete comic drama projects, manuscripts, scripts, setting assets, storyboards, and other creative data in the app, clear local data, or uninstall the app to remove all information. Because the current version does not provide an online account system, most rights related to access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal can be exercised locally on the device. If you have questions about this policy, data handling, or permission use, you may contact us through the email provided in this policy.

## 8. Minors

If you are under the age of 14, you should use the app only after your parent or legal guardian has read and agreed to this policy. We do not knowingly conduct targeted marketing toward minors or intentionally collect personal information from minors that is unrelated to the app's functions.

## 9. Updates and Contact

If there is a material change to product features, processing purposes, data types, permission use, or applicable rules, we will update this policy and provide notice in the app or on a prominent page.
